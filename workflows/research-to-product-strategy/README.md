# Workflow: Research to Product Strategy

## Overview
Takes market research findings and synthesizes them into a product strategy — positioning, differentiation, and go-to-market direction.

**Phases covered:** Discovery (research synthesis)
**Agents involved:** Research Analyst, Product Analyst
**Entry point:** Market research is complete
**Exit point:** Approved product strategy informing the value proposition

## Steps

### 1. Competitive Positioning
Using the competitor matrix from research:
- Map competitors on axes (e.g., simplicity vs. power, price vs. features)
- Identify white space — where no competitor is strong
- Recommend positioning for our product

**HITL:** "Here's where competitors sit. Here's the gap I see for us. Does this resonate?"

### 2. Differentiation Strategy
- What can we do that competitors can't or won't?
- Is our differentiation defensible (not easily copied)?
- Is it meaningful to the target users (not just technically interesting)?

Types of differentiation:
- Feature (we do X that nobody else does)
- Experience (we make X dramatically easier)
- Price (we serve the market at a different price point)
- Focus (we serve a specific niche better than generalists)

**HITL:** "Here's our differentiation angle. Strong enough?"

### 3. Target Segment Refinement
- Based on positioning, which segment should we capture first?
- Niche down — it's better to be loved by 100 users than liked by 10,000
- Define the beachhead market

**HITL:** "This is the audience we go after first. Agreed?"

### 4. Go-to-Market Direction
Not a full GTM plan, but directional:
- How will target users discover this product?
- What's the primary acquisition channel?
- Pricing direction (free, freemium, paid, enterprise)

**HITL:** "Here's the initial direction. We'll refine as we build."

## Outputs
| Output | Destination | Approval Required |
|---|---|---|
| Competitive positioning map | Notion/Confluence | Yes |
| Differentiation strategy | Notion/Confluence | Yes |
| Beachhead market definition | Notion/Confluence | Yes |
| GTM direction | Notion/Confluence | Yes |

## Notes
- This workflow runs within Discovery, not as a separate phase
- It's optional for projects where the founder already has strong positioning
- The outputs feed into the value proposition (product-discovery skill, Step 5)
